The Challenge There is no unified internal tool today. Each operations team—Customer Support, Credit Ops, Fraud Ops, and Compliance—works in its own separate dashboard or platform, built or adopted independently to serve that team’s specific needs. There is no shared customer profile, no cross-team visibility, and no common audit trail. Several teams also rely on external vendor platforms that are completely disconnected from each other. Teams context-switch between platforms, manually copy information, and maintain separate workflows in each. The company is targeting 10× transaction growth over the next 18 months while simultaneously expanding its product portfolio. The new products include Wallets and expansion of Credits Deliverable 5 - 7 Slide Presentation Task Given these goals and constraints, propose how Felix Pago should evolve its internal tooling strategy to support the next phase of growth. Your presentation should address Strategic Context & Goals ● Agent output must scale with 10x transaction growth without linearly scaling headcount ● Support multi-product expansion (wallet, credit) without building separate ops stacks per product ● Reduce cross-team context-switching and manual information transfer that currently slows resolution times ● Maintain compliance and audit readiness as regulatory complexity grows Product Vision & Architecture

Appendix Context & Team Tasks Customer Support (CX) ● Uses a CX dashboard to manage remittance transactions, refunds, and support tickets. ● Can see transaction status but not the reason for holds. ● Lacks visibility into compliance, fraud, credit, wallet balance, and case history. ● Relies on manual outreach to other teams for context. Compliance Operations ● Uses a compliance dashboard and KYC third party tool for KYC approvals ● Reviews documents, applies regulatory holds, and manages audit trails. ● Hold reasons and required user actions are not visible to CX. ● No cross-tool notifications when holds are placed or resolved. ● Triages with external disbursement providers exchanging transactional information Fraud Operations ● Uses a fraud dashboard and third-party risk tools to investigate flagged transactions. ● Approves or blocks transactions within fraud systems only. ● Fraud decisions are not visible to CX or Compliance. Credit Operations ● Uses spreadsheets and a payment platform to manage loans and repayments. ● Loan workflows are largely manual. ● Credit data is not connected to remittance activity or CX tools. Other Teams That Need Access ● Product ○ Reviews individual user journeys to understand customer experience. ○ Investigates bugs, edge cases, and pulls qualitative insights from real user data. ● Growth ○ Applies bulk actions to user cohorts (appeasements, promotions). ○ Analyzes user-level data for retention, activation, and conversion.
